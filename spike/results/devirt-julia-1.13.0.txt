## 5. Real rules, end to end (totals -- body allocation included)
   Each gets its own function on purpose. Looping and closing over the node would
   capture it as a `DataType` rather than `Type{NormalMeanVariance}`, which alone
   makes the call dynamic and reports `Any` -- a fourth way to mismeasure this.
   A real factor node has its form fixed, so the dedicated function is the honest
   model. (`typeof(+)` is immune: a function is already its own singleton type.)
